How the analyzer works

It reads the candles of the exchange that lists the coin (Binance futures first, then Bybit, Gate.io and Hyperliquid) on four timeframes, and applies the same rules every time:

  • Trend first. The daily and 4-hour trends (moving averages and the sequence of swing highs and lows) carry the most weight. A trade with the bigger trend needs less to go right.
  • Levels. Support and resistance zones come from swing points on every timeframe, the price where most volume traded, yesterday's and last week's highs and lows. Zones hit by several of them are stronger.
  • Setups. A breakout retest, a pullback to support (or rally into resistance), a squeeze breakout, a stop run (price pokes through a swing point and snaps back), or buying a dip at the next support.
  • Risk. The stop goes beyond the level that proves the idea wrong, never closer than one average candle range. Targets are the next zones ahead, at least 1 times the risk away. The size is set so the stop costs your chosen share of the account.
  • Positioning. Funding, open interest and long/short ratios show what traders do with money. Crowded longs or leverage chasing a move count against a long, as they did in our tests.
  • Tested score and sentiment. For coins on your DEXs, the 3-year-tested swing score from the Picks page and the free social sentiment score are added.

What the tests say. We replayed these exact rules on a year of hourly data for 118 coins (Sep 2025 to Oct 2026). At each step the analyzer saw only the data available then, and its plan was traded with the entry, stop, targets, partial profits and time stop shown on the page, after 0.12% round-trip costs. On 1-hour charts, 29,039 trades came out about break-even before fees and lost 0.04 times the risk per trade after them (42% winners). On 4-hour charts, 8,400 trades did much the same (−0.02 times the risk). A higher agreement number did not help, and no simple filter (trend alignment, RSI extremes, strong zones, stop runs) held up in both halves of the year.

That is normal for chart reading, and it is why the page shows the evidence first. The swing score from the Picks page did hold up over three years: longs scoring 80–89 made +2.8% per trade after fees, and shorts scoring 90+ made +5.1%. The best use of the analyzer: when a coin has a tested signal, use the plan here for the entry, the stop and the size. When it has none, treat the plan as risk control, not as a reason to trade.

Educational tool, not financial advice. Crypto perps can lose more than you expect: use the stop, size every trade by its risk, and never trade money you need.
