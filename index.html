    About this dashboard

    Every crypto perp listed on Hyperliquid, Variational, Aster, edgeX, Lighter, dYdX, Paradex and Extended is scanned each hour by nine strategies. Every signal is paper-traded after fees, slippage and funding: strategies that make money pass, losing ones are rejected, and the mistakes behind losing trades change how new signals are scored. Every day the scanner also invents new strategy variants, backtests them over 30 days and live-tests the best. Set your balance and risk under Your plan on the Picks tab and each pick shows your position size. The page checks for a new scan every 3 minutes.

    How it works

    1. Universe. The market list of every DEX (Hyperliquid, Variational, Aster, edgeX, Lighter, dYdX, Paradex, Extended) merged into one coin list. 1000PEPE, kPEPE and PEPE count as one coin; a DEX whose price is far from the main market for the same ticker is treated as a different asset and left out. Stocks, FX, indices, commodities and pre-IPO markets are removed.
    2. Stage 1, every coin. 300 hours (12.5 days) of 1-hour candles from MEXC futures, falling back to Gate.io, Bitget, Hyperliquid or Aster (so DEX-only coins are covered). A momentum score ranks them: 3h/6h/24h returns, strength against BTC, position in the 24h range, EMA8/21 trend, volume surge and trend smoothness, with penalties for overheated charts. The same candles give the 4-hour trend: up when price is above the 4h EMA20 and the EMA20 above the EMA50, down when both are the other way round, mixed otherwise.
    3. Stage 2, the leaders. 24 hours of 15-minute candles for the top 40 that trade at least $20K a day on some DEX, plus up to 16 more: coins Hyperliquid’s top traders hold long and 1-hour uptrends that are dipping. Indicators: EMA 9/21/50, RSI 14, MACD, ADX, ATR, Bollinger squeeze, 24h VWAP, swing structure and volume spikes.
    4. Nine strategies. Range breakout retest, squeeze break, EMA21 trend pullback, trend continuation (EMA9 dip), VWAP reclaim, oversold bounce in an uptrend, smart-money follow, 24-hour high breakout and EMA9/21 bull cross. Each sets its own entry zone, a stop under the level that proves it wrong (0.9 to 3.5 ATR, at most 5% away) and three targets in R. Their rules have settings (the RSI band, how close to the EMA, the volume needed...) that strategy discovery can change. The Strategy lab lists the exact rules.
    5. Smart money. Hyperliquid positions are public. The scanner takes its public leaderboard, keeps the 150 most profitable accounts over 30 days (at least $50K, profitable all-time, market makers removed) and reads their open positions: how many are long or short each coin, with how much money, at what average entry, and what changed since the last scan. Gate.io’s top-trader ratio, open-interest change and funding across DEXs add to it.
    6. Conviction score. Trend 20, momentum 20, volume 15, structure 15, positioning 15, liquidity 10 and market backdrop 5, minus penalties for overextension, late pumps, chasing, risk-off markets and going against the 4-hour downtrend. Then the journal adjusts it: a strategy that passed the tournament adds up to 8, one on watch loses points, lessons from past mistakes add or subtract, and a coin that just stopped out is cooled down. A pick needs a final score of 50. 75+ is High, 62-75 Medium.
    7. Journal. Every signal of every strategy is paper-traded: filled in the zone (or on the trigger close, or a buy-stop for reclaims) within 3 hours, a third off at each target, stop to breakeven after TP1, stop counted first if a candle touches both, closed at market after 12 hours. Costs come off every trade: a 0.045% taker fee plus 0.05-0.2% slippage (less on liquid coins) for market entries, stops and closes, a 0.015% maker fee for limit entries and targets, and the coin’s funding while the trade is open. For each trade it records the best run (MFE) and worst dip (MAE) in R, whether a stopped trade went on to hit TP1 within 3 hours (a stop hunt), and tags what went wrong.
    8. Strategy tournament. A strategy is tested until it has 15 closed trades. It passes with a clearly positive R per trade, a profit factor of 1.2 or more and either a 45%+ win rate or winners 1.5x the size of its losers. It is rejected when it clearly loses (negative R per trade or a profit factor under 0.8) and stops being published, but keeps being paper-traded so it can come back. Everything in between is on watch. Only the last 30 days count, so the verdicts follow the market.
    9. Tuning. Each strategy’s targets move toward the levels its trades actually reach (TP1 where about 55% of them got to, TP2 35%, TP3 20%) once 30 trades back them, and its stop widens by 10% when a third or more of recent stops were hunted, or tightens when winners rarely dip.
    10. Backtest. On the first run the scanner downloads 30 days of 15-minute candles for the 150 most traded coins and walks through them hour by hour, doing what a live scan does with only the candles that had closed by then, so the tournament starts with about a month of trades. Smart-money follow needs live positions, so it starts from zero. Backtest trades are marked in the journal and stop counting after 30 days.
    11. Strategy discovery. Once a day it creates 16 new variants of the strategies (one or two rule changes, an extra filter, other targets or a different stop), backtests each over the last 30 days next to its parent, and sends the few that clearly beat their parent after costs to a live test. Trying many variants on the same history always finds some that look good by luck, so the backtest only nominates: a variant is published only after it passes the tournament on at least 20 live trades spread over at least 5 days and does at least as well as its parent over the same days. It is retired when it loses money live or has not passed within 21 days.
    12. Your plan and goal. Position sizes come from the balance and risk per trade you enter (saved on this device only): position = balance × risk ÷ (distance to the stop + fees and slippage in and out), capped by your maximum leverage, so a stop-out loses about the amount you chose. The goal tracker compares your logged balance with the path to your goal and replays the journal’s own trade results at random (a Monte Carlo simulation) to show the range of outcomes at different risk levels.
    13. Alerts (optional). With a Telegram bot or a Discord webhook added to the repository’s secrets, each scan sends new picks scoring 62 or more. The README explains the setup.

    Educational tool, not financial advice. Paper-trading results include estimated fees, funding and slippage, but real fills differ, and past results do not predict future ones. Perpetual futures are leveraged and can lose more than you put in. Levels are per 1 coin; where a DEX lists a multiple (1000PEPE, kPEPE) multiply by the number shown. Chart prices come from the most liquid source and differ slightly between venues, so check the live price on your DEX before you trade. Data: public APIs of Hyperliquid, Variational, Aster, edgeX, Lighter, dYdX, Paradex, Extended, MEXC, Gate.io and Bitget.
