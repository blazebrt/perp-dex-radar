How the call is made

Only tested signals make the call. A coin gets LONG or SHORT only when one of these is live on it. Everything else is a wait.

  • A Coin picks swing score of 80 or more. Three-year test on 118 coins (Oct 2023 to Oct 2026): +$29 per $100 risked over 5,453 trades after fees, positive every year for longs and shorts. Exits as tested: in at the daily open, stop 2 daily ATR (kept between 5% and 25%), trailing stop 3 ATR behind the best price, out after 30 days at the latest.
  • A position of the quant desk. Its three live strategies made +$9 per $100 risked over 4,514 trades in three years after fees, slippage and funding, and did better than random entries taken at the same moments. Exits: the desk's own stop, trailing stop and time limit.
  • Proven traders shorting together: 2 or more of Hyperliquid's proven traders opening shorts on the same coin within 24 hours. Promising, not proven: +$14 per $100 risked over 31 trades in a 30-day test without hindsight, so it is traded at half size until 40 live trades decide. Exits: stop 1.5 times the coin's typical daily move, out after 24 hours.

The plan is the tested trade. You join each trade as it stands (same stop level, same trailing stop, same time limit), so the trade on this page is the one the records count. There are no profit targets: the tested exits let the winners run, and the winners pay for the losers. The size is set so the stop costs your chosen share of the account (half of it for promising and mixed signals).

When signals disagree. Proven signals on both sides: no trade. When several agree, the plan follows the one with the strongest record. A weaker signal against a proven one is shown but does not change the call.

The chart read is context. Trend, levels, momentum and positioning on four timeframes show where the price sits and which levels matter. We tested whether it makes the calls better: on the 5,453 swing signals of the last three years, trades made +$27 per $100 risked when the 4-hour read agreed and +$38 when it disagreed (+$18 against +$45 on the daily chart), and swing shorts did worst when the 4-hour read agreed (−$1). So the chart read never confirms or blocks a call. On its own its plans were about break-even before fees and lost money after them (29,039 trades on 1-hour charts and 8,400 on 4-hour charts, one year, 118 coins).

No guarantee. Between 38% and 61% of these trades win, depending on the signal. The edge is small and shows over many trades, not in any one of them. Expect losing runs, risk the same small share every time, and never move a stop away from the price.

Educational tool, not financial advice. Crypto perps can lose more than you expect: use the stop, size every trade by its risk, and never trade money you need.
